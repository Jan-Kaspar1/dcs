# Batch-control requirements

Batch control for pharmaceutical and chemical plants is deferred until the water and wastewater foundation and first pilot workflow are proven.

- **Status:** deferred
- **Revisit condition:** a water/wastewater reference application satisfies its accepted requirements, pilot-readiness gaps are documented, and target batch customers or domain experts supply evidence for a separate requirements baseline.

Future research may examine recipe management, procedural control, unit allocation, material tracking, electronic records, and applicable standards. These subjects are extension pressures for architecture review, not current implementation scope.
