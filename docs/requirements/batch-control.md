# Batch-control requirements

Batch control for chemical and pharmaceutical plants is deferred. The market sequence in `docs/market-roadmap.md` places chemical and pharmaceutical after water and wastewater, food and beverage, and cement. Research may prepare a later stage, but implementation remains parked until that stage is reached and its evidence gate is met.

- **Status:** deferred
- **Revisit condition:** the preceding market-stage gates have passed; target chemical or pharmaceutical customers or domain experts provide evidence for a separate requirements baseline; and a representative batch application is selected with observable acceptance scenarios. Chemical and pharmaceutical need distinct evidence packs even if some accepted capabilities overlap.

Future research may examine recipe management, procedural control, unit allocation, material tracking, electronic records, and applicable standards. These are questions for the relevant customer- and domain-supported evidence pack, not current implementation scope or claims about every plant in either market.

The [Maravelias / ISA-88 investigation](../research/chemical-production-scheduling.md), preserved for [issue #1531](https://github.com/Jan-Kaspar1/dcs/issues/1531), records the accessible primary sources, design alternatives, and candidate requirements `CPS-C01`–`CPS-C13`. Its proposed separation of scheduling, admission/execution, equipment capability and resource ownership is review input, not an accepted batch-engine design. Promote candidates only with a representative application, domain-owner evidence and measurable acceptance scenarios. The full-book/standards access limits and missing distinct scheduling report remain explicit; this research does not pass a market gate.
