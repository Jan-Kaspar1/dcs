# Product research

Research notes provide evidence for requirements and planning. One note covers one bounded question or coherent source set.

Each note records:

- the question and affected requirement IDs;
- source title, publisher, version or date, URL, and pages or sections used;
- facts supported by each source;
- proposed DCS implications, clearly labeled as proposals;
- assumptions and questions that require customer validation;
- the date the sources were checked.

Prefer primary sources: standards publishers, regulators, vendor manuals, owner specifications, and direct operator or integrator evidence. Vendor manuals are useful design references but do not define this product. Summarize behavior and rationale in original language and retain precise citations; do not copy source text, diagrams, faceplates, names, or protected visual assets into the product.

A research issue is complete when the note and affected requirements agree, every claim is traceable, open assumptions are visible, and the planner can either write measurable acceptance criteria or defer the requirement with a concrete revisit condition.
